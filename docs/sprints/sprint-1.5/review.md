# Review de UIUX

## General

The refresh button could be bigger or at the very least a primary button.


## Session

- The "Updated X min ago" label should be either inside the expanded view or below the refresh button
- The session area should be split into 2: Session Type (Practice) and Race Track (Brands Hatch)
- The Race Track should have its corresponding flag
- The car should also have the year in a badge
- The setup could have a tachometer style of showing the values
- Conditions should be renamed to Weather
- Air and Track temp should be rearranged
- Weather icons should have colors or something (nice feedback alex)

## Live lap

- Lap 6/20 should be only Lap 6 (no total) if in practice session
- Live delta should be closer to the lap time, and with a filled background
- The badge "invalid" or "track record" should switch with the delta (stay on the ceiling)
- Lap time should be bigger
- Revise time font
- Sector colors should also apply to the border and to the "S1" label

## Session laps

- Should be renamed to "Previous laps" or similar
- The "Recent First/Fastest First" choice should be a tab-like choice, almost like a switch
- The sort and view options should stay in the same row. hopefully with the same switch, tab-like style
- The track/car separators should have text centered
- Sector times should loose the badge style: instead, use bottom border for the color and use white.
- Also, rework spacing with the header, to better align header with body cells
